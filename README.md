# James

I build local-first AI workflow tools, simulation systems, and practical self-hosted software.

My work sits at the intersection of product engineering and systems experimentation: turning complex local models and simulation engines into software that people can install, understand, and use.

## Selected work

### [Draft to Take](https://github.com/JaySpiffy/draft-to-take)

A local-first AI audio production studio for developing scripts, preparing voices, generating dialogue and sound, arranging timeline clips, and exporting finished mixes.

### [Void Reckoning](https://github.com/JaySpiffy/void-reckoning-engine)

A persistent 4X strategy simulation with evolving factions, tactical combat replays, a terminal HUD, and analytics, built with Python and Rust.

## Engineering focus

- Local AI workflows that keep creative data on the user's machine
- Persistent simulations and emergent systems
- Self-hosted applications and reproducible deployment
- Practical documentation, diagnostics, and release tooling

I value ambitious software that is honest about its current state, straightforward to evaluate, and steadily improved through real use.
