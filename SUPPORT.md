# Support my work

I build local-first creative tools, strategy simulations, game-development automation, and experimental AI-assisted development systems.

A lot of the work starts as a personal experiment: solve a difficult problem, prove it works, then turn the useful parts into something reusable. GitHub Sponsors helps me spend more time on that cycle.

## What sponsorship supports

Support goes toward my **original development work**, including:

- development and testing time;
- game-development automation and QA tooling;
- local AI workflow tools;
- reusable strategy/simulation systems;
- build, storage, release, and testing infrastructure;
- documentation and public examples;
- rights-cleared original projects and reusable open tooling where licensing allows it.

## What sponsorship does not buy

Sponsorship does **not** buy access to third-party copyrighted assets, proprietary game content, private credentials, model weights I do not own, or rights to somebody else's intellectual property.

Some of my private research uses existing software or content as a technical compatibility/research target. Those projects can demonstrate the tooling, but they are not being sold through sponsorship.

## Ways to help

### Sponsor

If you have the spare money and want to help me keep building, you can support me through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/JaySpiffy).

There is no expectation that people sponsor just to use my public work.

### Help without spending money

Stars, useful bug reports, testing feedback, sharing a demo, or simply showing a project to somebody who might find it useful all help.

## Current public projects

### [Draft to Take](https://github.com/JaySpiffy/draft-to-take)

A local-first script-to-audio production studio for writing/importing scripts, preparing voices, generating and reviewing takes, adding sound design, arranging a timeline, and exporting finished mixes.

### [Void Reckoning Engine](https://github.com/JaySpiffy/void-reckoning-engine)

An experimental persistent 4X campaign and combat simulation framework focused on deterministic simulation, strategic AI, campaign state, reporting, and reusable systems.

## Why I use AI-assisted development

AI lets one person attempt projects that would otherwise need much more time or a larger team. I still care about whether the result is correct: tests, reproducible evidence, explicit state ownership, failed-attempt preservation, and real player/user paths matter more to me than simply generating a lot of code.

If that kind of development is interesting to you, following the work is already appreciated. Sponsorship is just a way to help me spend more time on it.
